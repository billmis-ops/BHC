Merrill FAQs

Practical questions about this location.

Contact our team if your question is not answered here.

Can I contact BHC without a referral?

Yes. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment. A primary care referral is not required.

What services does this clinic provide?

BHC provides therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Can I reach out if I am not sure what kind of care I need?

Yes. Tell us what has been difficult, and our team can explain the therapy, assessment and medication-management options that may be relevant.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

BHC accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on your plan and service. View accepted insurance plans.

Can appointments be in person or online?

Yes. Appointments are available at the clinic and online. The format available to you depends on the service.

Where is the Merrill clinic located?

The clinic is at 100 S Mill St, Suite 104, Merrill, WI 54452. Open directions in Google Maps.
