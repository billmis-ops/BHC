Wausau FAQs

Questions about this location.

Contact our team if you need information not listed here.

Do I need a referral to start care at BHC?

No. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment. A referral from a primary care provider is not required.

What mental health services are available here?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

What if I am not sure which type of care I need?

You can still request an appointment. Tell us what is concerning you, and our team can explain available therapy, assessment and medication-management options.

Does BHC accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs vary by plan, provider and service. View accepted insurance plans.

Are in-person and online appointments available?

Yes. BHC offers appointments at the Wausau clinic and online. The format available to you depends on the service.

Where is the Behavioral Health Clinic in Wausau?

The clinic is at 630 S 36th Ave, Wausau, WI 54401. Open directions in Google Maps.
