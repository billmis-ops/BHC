Eagle River FAQs

Answers for planning virtual care.

Age group, services, insurance and what to include in an appointment request.

Is Eagle River mental health care provided online?

Yes. BHC's Eagle River service is designed for virtual appointments.

Who does the Eagle River service support?

The Eagle River service supports children and adolescents under 18.

What services are available through Eagle River virtual care?

BHC provides virtual therapy, assessments and medication management for children and adolescents.

Can I request care without a referral?

Yes. A parent or caregiver can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request care.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What should I include when requesting virtual care for a child or teen?

Share the child or teen's age, the main concern, what you have noticed and insurance information. You do not need to select a clinician or service first.

What happens after I send an appointment request?

The team will review the information and follow up about the relevant care option and appointment offered.
