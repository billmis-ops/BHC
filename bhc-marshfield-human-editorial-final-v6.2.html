Marshfield FAQs

Questions people ask before reaching out.

Clear answers about services, age groups, insurance and the Marshfield office.

Do I need a referral to contact the Marshfield clinic?

No. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment at the Marshfield office.

What services and age groups does the Marshfield clinic serve?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

What happens after I request an appointment?

The BHC team reviews the information you share, contacts you about relevant care options, and provides the details for the appointment offered.

Can I meet with someone in person or online?

BHC provides care at the East Third Street office and online. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

Where is the Marshfield Behavioral Health Clinic?

The Marshfield office is at 601 E 3rd St, Marshfield, WI 54449. Look for the Behavioral Health Clinic sign outside the building. Open directions in Google Maps.
