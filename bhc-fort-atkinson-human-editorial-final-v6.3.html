Fort Atkinson FAQs

Practical answers before you contact the clinic.

Services, appointment formats, insurance and finding Suite 409.

Can I request care in Fort Atkinson without a referral?

Yes. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request care at the Fort Atkinson office.

What services and age groups are supported in Fort Atkinson?

BHC offers counseling, psychological testing and assessments, medication management, affirming and neurodiverse counseling, relationship and sex therapy, and speech, language, feeding and swallowing services. Services support children, teens and adults.

What appointment formats are offered?

BHC provides in-person care at Suite 409, 201 N Main St, and online appointments. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What should I include when I request an appointment?

Share the main concern, the age of the person seeking care, insurance information, and the preferred appointment format. You do not need to choose a clinician or service first.

Where is the Fort Atkinson clinic?

The clinic is in Suite 409 at 201 N Main St, Fort Atkinson, WI 53538. Office hours are Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.
