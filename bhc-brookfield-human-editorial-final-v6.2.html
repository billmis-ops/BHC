Brookfield FAQs

Answers for planning your first contact.

Care options, insurance, appointment requests and practical office details.

Can I contact the Brookfield clinic without a referral?

Yes. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment at the Brookfield office.

What services and age groups are supported in Brookfield?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Are in-person and online appointments available?

BHC provides appointments at the Regency Court office and online. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What information should I include in an appointment request?

Share the main concern, the age of the person seeking care, insurance information, and whether in-person or online care is preferred. You do not need to select a clinician first.

Where is the Brookfield clinic and what are the office hours?

The Brookfield clinic is in Suite 200A at 245 Regency Ct, Brookfield, WI 53045. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.
