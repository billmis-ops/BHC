Who We Help

Sports psychology is for everyone connected to the game.

Whether you compete, coach, parent, or support athletes clinically, BHC can help address the emotional and mental side of performance.

Athlete

Student athletes balancing sports, school, and stress

Support for focus, pressure, time demands, confidence, and emotional regulation during busy seasons.

Performance

Athletes managing performance anxiety

Tools to manage nerves, reset after mistakes, and stay grounded during practices, games, and competitions.

Recovery

Players recovering mentally after injury

Care for the emotional side of injury, motivation, identity, and confidence while working back to play.

Parents

Parents noticing burnout or pressure

Guidance when a young athlete seems overwhelmed, anxious, withdrawn, frustrated, or unlike themselves.

Coaches

Coaches looking for stronger mental performance tools

A trusted resource for athletes who may need more support than encouragement or practice alone can provide.

Providers

Physical therapists supporting the whole athlete

Referral support for athletes whose recovery includes confidence, fear of reinjury, or mental health concerns.

Performance is never only physical. Athletes often carry pressure people around them never fully see — expectations, injury recovery, burnout, confidence struggles, anxiety, and the constant push to keep going.

Why It Matters

The mental game affects confidence, recovery, and wellbeing.

Sports psychology creates space to build healthier mental habits, emotional resilience, and sustainable performance — both in sport and in life.
