What We Treat

Comprehensive mental health care under one roof.

From therapy and psychiatric medication management to psychological assessments and specialized support, BHC offers care for a wide range of needs.

Therapy

Individual Therapy

One-on-one support for anxiety, depression, trauma, stress, adjustment challenges, and more.

Psychiatry

Psychiatric Services

Evaluation, diagnosis, medication management, and ongoing psychiatric care from experienced providers.

Assessments

Family Support
