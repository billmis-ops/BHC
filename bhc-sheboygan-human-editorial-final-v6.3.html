Sheboygan FAQs

What to know before contacting the clinic.

Clear answers about care, appointments and visiting the North 9th Street office.

Can I request care in Sheboygan without a referral?

Yes. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request care at the Sheboygan office.

What services and age groups does the Sheboygan clinic support?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Can appointments be in person or online?

BHC provides in-person care at 703 N 9th St and online appointments. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What should I include when I request an appointment?

Share the main concern, the age of the person seeking care, insurance information, and whether in-person or online care is preferred. You do not need to choose a clinician or service first.

Where is the Sheboygan clinic, and what should I know before visiting?

The clinic is at 703 N 9th St, Sheboygan, WI 53081. It is open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM and closed Saturday and Sunday. Appointments are required. Free on-site parking and wheelchair-accessible parking, entrance and restroom are available.
