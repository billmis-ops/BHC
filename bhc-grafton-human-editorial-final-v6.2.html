Grafton FAQs

Practical answers before you contact the clinic.

What is available, what to share and how to plan a visit.

Do I need a referral to request care in Grafton?

No. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment at the Grafton office.

What mental health services are available in Grafton?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Can I meet with someone in person or online?

BHC provides appointments at the Cheyenne Avenue office and online. The format offered depends on the service and clinician.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What if I am not sure which type of care I need?

Describe the concern and how it is affecting daily life. The team can explain whether therapy, an assessment or medication management may be relevant before an appointment is scheduled.

Where is the Grafton clinic and what are the office hours?

The clinic is in Suite 200 at 1245 Cheyenne Ave, Grafton, WI 53024. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.
