Madison FAQs

Before you contact the Madison clinic.

Short answers to common questions about care, appointments and this office.

Can I contact the Madison clinic without a referral?

Yes. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment at the Madison clinic. A referral from a primary care provider is not required.

What services and age groups does the Madison clinic serve?

BHC provides therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Are both in-person and online appointments available?

Yes. BHC offers appointments at the Cottage Grove Road clinic and online. The format available depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What happens after I request an appointment?

The BHC team reviews your concerns and appointment preferences, explains the relevant options, and contacts you about appointment details.

Where is the Madison Behavioral Health Clinic?

The clinic is in Suite 100 at 4706 Cottage Grove Rd, Madison, WI 53716. The roadside sign displays Behavioral Health Clinic and the street number 4706. Open directions in Google Maps.
