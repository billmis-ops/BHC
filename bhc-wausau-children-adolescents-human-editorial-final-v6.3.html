Wausau youth care FAQs

Answers for parents and caregivers.

Services, appointment formats, insurance and finding the Grant Street center.

Can I request care for my child without a referral?

Yes. A parent or caregiver can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request care through the Wausau Center for Children and Adolescents.

Who does the Wausau Center for Children and Adolescents serve?

The center focuses on mental health care for children and adolescents.

What services are available through the center?

BHC provides therapy, assessments and medication management for children and adolescents.

Are online appointments available?

Yes. BHC offers care at the Grant Street center and online. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What should I include when requesting care for a child or teen?

Share the child or teen's age, the main concern, what you have noticed, insurance information and whether in-person or online care is preferred. You do not need to select a clinician or service first.

Where is the Wausau Center for Children and Adolescents?

The center is located at 530 Grant St, Wausau, WI 54403.
