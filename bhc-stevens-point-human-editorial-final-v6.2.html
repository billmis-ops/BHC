Stevens Point FAQs

Practical details before you contact the clinic.

Services, appointments, insurance, location and hours in one place.

Do I need a referral to contact the Stevens Point clinic?

No. You can contact Behavioral Health Clinic directly to request an appointment at the Stevens Point office.

What services and age groups does the Stevens Point clinic serve?

BHC offers therapy, psychological and diagnostic assessments, and medication management for children, teens and adults.

Can I receive care in person or online?

BHC provides appointments at the Post Road office and online. The available format depends on the service.

Does Behavioral Health Clinic accept insurance?

Behavioral Health Clinic accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan and service. View the current accepted insurance plans.

What happens after I send an appointment request?

The BHC team reviews the information you share, contacts you about relevant care options, and provides the details for the appointment offered.

Where is the Stevens Point office and when is it open?

The clinic is at 2417 Post Rd, Stevens Point, WI 54481. Office hours are Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.
